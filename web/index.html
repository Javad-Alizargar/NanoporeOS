Steady ion transport in a cylindrical nanopore

Axisymmetric steady Poisson–Nernst–Planck in a cylindrical channel. All outputs are synthetic numerical results. A converged solve alone does not establish mesh accuracy or experimental validity.

Scenario

288 cells · 864 coupled unknowns. One solve at a time · ≤648 cells · 60 s limit · ≤10 queued jobs. Fields have no physical time coordinate.

Equations, boundaries and limits

−∇·(ε∇φ) = F(c₊−c₋); ∇·Jᵢ = 0; Jᵢ = −Dᵢ[∇cᵢ + zᵢcᵢ∇φ/Vₜ]. Temperature 298.15 K; εᵣ 78.3; D₊ 1.96×10⁻⁹ and D₋ 2.03×10⁻⁹ m²/s.

End reservoirs prescribe salt and potentials 0 and V. Wall φ(x) = Vx/L + offset; ions cannot cross the wall. The wall offset is a prescribed potential, without an established experimental zeta interpretation. Axis symmetry. No access resistance.

Unavailable: fluid coupling, molecular feedback, transient PNP, calibrated acquisition, experimental validation, qualified surrogate predictions.
